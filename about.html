About the tutor

Concord’s as your Tutor

A local-first study companion that turns your own notes into practice questions, gives immediate feedback, and keeps a simple record of your quiz performance.

🔒

Privacy-minded

Profile, quiz history and extracted study text are stored in your browser. This static build does not upload files to a remote server.

🧠

Material-based

The question builder uses sentences and concepts found in your material. It does not intentionally invent outside subject matter.

🌗

Study anywhere

Responsive pages, dark/light mode and normal HTML links make the app usable across phones and computers.
