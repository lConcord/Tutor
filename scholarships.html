Scholarships and opportunities

Only verified opportunities are listed: each one was checked against its official website on 25 November 2026. Deadlines change every year, so always read the official notice before you apply.

How to spot a fake scholarship

  • Use the official website only. Type the address yourself. Copycat sites exist, and even the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission has warned about a look-alike organisation.
  • Never pay to apply, and never pay an agent for a “guaranteed” place. Official scholarship bodies do not sell places.
  • Take deadlines and requirements from the official notice, not from social media posts or forwarded WhatsApp messages.
  • Do not send passports, certificates or ID documents to unofficial contacts.
  • If an offer sounds too good to be true, ask a teacher or school counsellor before you reply.
