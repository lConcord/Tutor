Public universities with their official websites. Entry requirements, cut-offs and deadlines change every year, so always read the official admissions page.

Stay safe when applying. Universities, including KNUST, have warned applicants about people who offer to “secure” admission or hall places for a fee. Use only the official website. You can also check that an institution is accredited with the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission at gtec.edu.gh.
